  • The evolutionary arm is ahead in every seed at 100 and 200 relaxations and at equal calls. It is not ahead at 50.
  • It samples less widely: about 157 unique structures per seed against 199, and 21% of its relaxations repeat a structure it had.
  • 451 distinct structures lie within 0.30 eV. Each evolutionary seed found 21–22% of them, each random seed 13–15%. Neither arm exhausts the surface in 200 relaxations.
  • Wall time is not compared: the runs shared the GPU with an unrelated job.

What the search exposed about the model

MACE-MPA-0 gives CO almost no binding to this cluster

46 relaxed structures have CO 6–7 Å away from the cluster. The lowest is 0.022 eV above the best bound structure. MACE-MATPES-r2SCAN puts the same structures 1.8–2.4 eV up. Neither has been checked against UBPW91.

  • The search flagged these structures and kept them out of the parent population. Two are on the reference shortlist because they measure the CO binding energy directly.
  • No CO dissociation, formyl, or H2 formation in 1,200 relaxations, and no atom overlaps. With a model that barely binds CO, the absence of CO activation says nothing about Fe16.
  • The two models order 72% of low-energy pairs the same way; the MPA-0 minimum is rank 28 for r2SCAN.

Reference shortlist

Twenty structures proposed for UBPW91 at M = 49, 47 and 45 (60 states). Energies are model values in eV above the MPA-0 pooled minimum, for both models. Not submitted.

Limits

  • Nothing here is a physical result for Fe16. It is not a statement about supported Fe/K on θ-alumina or a working catalyst.
  • One geometry, one composition, Fe fixed at the UBPW91 positions, which are not a minimum of the model.
  • ACAT's own site finder and genetic operators were not exercised; equivalent operators were written against the project's site list.
  • Recovery of DFT-confirmed motifs could not be measured.
