# Foundation-transfer pilot: specification (2026-10-27)

**Status: proposed.** Nothing here has been run except the inexpensive check in §7. Training, data
collection and DFT need separate authorization, and the HPC parts go through the dispatch desk. The
context is in `docs/grace-seminar-assessment-2026-10-27.md`.

## 1. Question and falsification

**Question:** at a matched training budget, does starting from a pretrained foundation model give
lower *held-out, independent-DFT* errors than the from-scratch charge/multiplicity MACE control? The
errors that count are the ones the target chemistry needs: relative energies within a state, spin
gaps, adsorption energies, and forces on Fe and N/H.

**Transfer benefit is falsified** if every one of the following holds:

1. With 3 seeds per arm, the fine-tune's independent-test Fe force RMSE and N2/H adsorption-energy
   MAE are not lower than the scratch control's by more than the seed-to-seed spread, at any of the
   three data fractions (25/50/100 %).
2. The fine-tune is worse than scratch on any (q, M) class, or on spin-gap sign ordering. A transfer
   that trades spin physics for a lower average counts as a failure, not a wash.
3. The fine-tune's saved model fails the embedding check (it does not respond to M). The arm is then
   invalid, not negative, and is re-run after the fix.

A benefit at 25 % that vanishes at 100 % is reported as "data-efficiency only". That is the
seminar's claim (Q3) and is a legitimate, narrower result.

## 2. Arms (matched budget)

| **G** (optional, single-state) | GRACE-2L-OMAT-medium-ft-E (or -SMAX-OMAT-medium) fine-tuned with gracemaker on **one (q, M) at a time** | None; the model is state-blind. It is fed only frames of one declared state | Tests GRACE fairly where conditioning is not needed. Skip it if F shows no transfer |
| **Z** (reference only) | Zero-shot MACE-MP-0 small, GRACE-2L-OMAT, MACE-OMOL-0 | MACE-OMOL gets native q/M. The others have none | Shows how far each start point is from UBPW91 (seminar Q11: "measure the mismatch first") |

Matched budget: identical train/valid/test files; the same number of optimizer updates (not epochs)
for S and F, chosen from S's validation curve; the same loss weights; the best-validation checkpoint
for both. F gets no extra hyperparameter search that S did not get.

**Why not POLAR-1 or MACE-OMOL as F:** they are native charge/spin but trained at ωB97M-V (a change
of method), and both failed or diverged on Fe16. They stay as zero-shot references (Z) on the small
clusters only.

## 3. Target reference and data

**Reference level:** UBPW91 at the campaign route (6-311++G*, UltraFine, IOP(5/13=1) in every link).
Frames are accepted only where the SCF actually converged. The label level comes from `label-levels`
(same_level only). VASP frames never enter S, F or G (delta-only decision).

**Chemistry (gas-phase models of the supported-cluster active site):** Fe_n, Fe_nN2 (side-on and
end-on) and Fe_nH_k (k ≤ 4), with n = 3–6, neutral and ±1, and every multiplicity on record for each
geometry.

**Sources (no new DFT for training):**

- legacy UBPW91 records for Fe_nN2 / Fe_nH_k (package 26 extraction on LONI, `seeds.extxyz`);
- the FenOm/General warehouses, filtered by formula;
- optimization trajectories collected with `--frames completed-stages`.

**Independent DFT test set (new, needs authorization):** about 150 fixed-geometry UBPW91 `Force`
single points that were never part of any training trajectory. Sample them from MLIP MD (S's
committee) at 300/600 K plus random rattles (σ = 0.05/0.10 Å) of held-out minima, at their declared
(q, M). For N2 adsorption, include the X, Fe_n and Fe_nX endpoints at the same level, so adsorption
energies are DFT-computed.

**Splits (prevent leakage):**

- A group is a source-geometry cluster by pair-distance fingerprint (≤ 0.01 Å). It includes all
  multiplicities of that geometry (ladder relatives), every frame of its optimization, and any
  rattles or teacher-generated siblings. A group never straddles splits.
- Hold out one whole composition per family (e.g. Fe6N2 and Fe5H2) as an extrapolation test.
- Report the numbers of frames and of independent geometries separately.

## 4. Commands (to run after authorization)

```bash
# 0. environment check
cluster-mlip doctor

# 1. collect at one label level (LONI qbd); repeat per source, then merge
cluster-mlip label-levels <source> --formula Fe3N2 ... -o levels_pilot
cluster-mlip collect <gaussian_jobs> -o pilot_ds --frames completed-stages \
  --stratify-by pes_region,charge_spin_class --split-group-tolerance 0.01

# 2. learning-curve subsets: 25/50/100 % of train *groups* (not frames); same valid/test

# 3. arms S and F, 3 seeds each, per fraction
cluster-mlip train pilot_ds_f100 -o runs/S_f100 --seed 1 --seed 2 --seed 3 \
  --forces-weight 10 --max-num-epochs <from S validation curve> --extra-arg=--lr=0.001
cluster-mlip train pilot_ds_f100 -o runs/F_f100 --finetune --foundation-model small \
  --seed 1 --seed 2 --seed 3 --forces-weight 10 --max-num-epochs <same updates as S>
#    then, before any further runs: load each F model, confirm joint_embedding and dE/dM != 0

# 4. evaluate on valid, independent test and composition hold-out
cluster-mlip evaluate pilot_test.extxyz --model runs/S_f100/seed_1/...model -o eval/S_f100_s1
cluster-mlip calibrate-committee pilot_test.extxyz --models runs/S_f100/seed_*/...model -o calib/S_f100

# 5. (optional arm G) gracemaker fine-tune per single (q, M) subset; predict with grace_predict
#    and score with the same evaluate code via an extxyz of predictions
```

`evaluate` reports per-element forces against the zero-force baseline (added 2026-10-27).
`calibrate-committee` is new and checks the selector against independent error (§7).

## 6. Proposed acceptance criteria (new thresholds, labelled *proposed*)

These are tied to the chemistry, with the measured reference uncertainty as the floor. Until the
label-noise floor (assessment action 6) is measured, use package 62/65 residuals as a provisional
scale, and do not claim agreement below it.

| Quantity | Proposed criterion | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Relative energies within a state | MAE ≤ 43 meV per cluster (1 kcal/mol) on the independent test set | Ensemble weights at 600–700 K change by ~2× per 43 meV |
| Spin-gap ordering | correct sign for every pair with \|gap\| > 0.1 eV; gap MAE ≤ 0.1 eV | Sampling inside a declared state is useless if the model mis-orders states the DFT separates |
| N2 / H adsorption energy | MAE ≤ 0.1 eV; correct side-on/end-on ordering when DFT separates them by > 0.05 eV | The package 27 zero-shot models failed exactly this (see that report) |
| Fe and N/H forces | RMSE ≤ 0.1 eV/Å **and** ≤ 50 % of the element's zero-force RMSE | Forces below the zero baseline are the minimum for MD; package 32 failed this on Fe and N |
| Committee | ρ ≥ 0.5 within unseen-geometry frames; top-10 % error recall ≥ 0.5; other-M frames reported separately | The selector must find real errors, not only novelty |

Thresholds come from these physical scales, not from the seminar's numbers. The B–W table, the
170× / 1.9× speed–error point and the Mendeleev CALM plot are not predictions for our systems or
hardware.

## 9. Distillation (specified only; gated)

- **Teacher:** whichever arm passes §6, and only within its validated scope: the compositions,
  charges, multiplicities and PES regions present in its independent test set. Nothing else.
- **Candidates:** MD from the teacher at 300–900 K within declared (q, M); rattles; N2/H approach
  scans. Spin and charge states absent from the DFT training data are **outside** the
  synthetic-label domain and are never generated.
- **Diversity selection:** farthest-point sampling on the teacher's invariant features, per element
  and state, with a committee/latent filter that discards candidates where the teacher itself is
  extrapolating.
- **Provenance:** synthetic frames carry `label_level = teacher:<model hash>`, never the DFT route.
  `train` would refuse to mix them with DFT unless asked (proposed check; not implemented). Every DFT
  frame of the teacher's training set is kept in the student's training set as an anchor.
- **Student:** a 1-interaction, smaller-channel MACE with the same q/M embeddings.
- **Reporting:** student-vs-teacher error (approximation) and teacher-vs-DFT error (inherited) are
  reported **separately** on the independent DFT test set. Teacher–student agreement is never
  reported as accuracy.
- **Amplification guard:** cap synthetic frames per DFT group, and check that student errors on
  held-out DFT compositions are no worse than the teacher's.

## 10. Uncertainty follow-up (specified)

- CALM itself is not usable on MACE: it is built into GRACE's basis. The portable idea is
  per-element Gaussian-mixture Mahalanobis distance on frozen MACE invariant node features, as in
  arXiv:2402.03753. Prototype only after the pilot data exist; the committee stays the baseline.
- Calibration set: the independent test set **plus** (a) a deliberately withheld composition, (b)
  seen geometries at withheld multiplicities, (c) MD frames beyond the training temperature. Report
  ρ and top-error recall per class, as in §7. A low grade is reported as "interpolating in the
  training features", never as "accurate".
